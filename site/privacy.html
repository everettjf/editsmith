Privacy Policy

EditSmith is designed to keep source text and automation actions on your Mac.

No developer-operated service

EditSmith does not send source text, actions, test fixtures, settings, or usage data to a server operated by the developer.

Local execution

Actions execute locally through JavaScriptCore. The Source Editor Extension receives only the text and selections supplied by XcodeKit when you invoke an action.

Local storage

Actions, settings, test fixtures, and the most recent undo snapshot are stored locally in the app's shared container.

No network entitlement

The app and extension do not request network access.

Questions may be sent to xnuapp@gmail.com.
